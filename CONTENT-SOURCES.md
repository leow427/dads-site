All fourteen home-page Trusted by logos are present. The original client list, program galleries, album links, two embedded YouTube videos, and original full-resolution press-photo links are retained. The advisory board is omitted as requested.

The homepage uses the original mission, vision, audience list, and descriptions. Navigation retains Home, Classes, Music, Art, Our Clients, and Press, with original child pages under Classes, Music, and Art. The `/new-page` URL is also available as an alias of the home page.

The demo player uses public Apple Music preview URLs returned by https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1411115393&entity=song&limit=50 . Audio is streamed, not copied into this project. Track artwork is from the original site, with the Guacamole single artwork supplied by the same public music metadata.
